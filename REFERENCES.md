# NO LOGO — references and site direction

Streetwear brand from Syria, selling on Instagram today. The site should feel like the big streetwear stores but simpler. Final colours and fonts wait for the logo.

## Reference sites (checked live, Nov 2026)

| Site | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| [Stüssy](https://www.stussy.com) | The simplest of all. Logo top-left, four text links (Filter, Search, Bag, Menu), 2-column grid of big product photos, name + price under each in small caps. **Main reference for the shop grid.** |
| [Cole Buxton](https://www.colebuxton.com) | Full-screen product photo as the hero, no text on it. Grid mixes flat product shots with on-model shots. Name left, price right, colour under the name. |
| [Fear of God](https://fearofgod.com) | Centered wordmark header, one full-screen campaign photo with two buttons (Discover / Shop). Best mobile grid: 2 columns, item count, Filter + Sort in one line. **Main reference for mobile.** |
| [Represent](https://representclo.com) | Dark, moody campaign photography. Good reference if the logo pushes us to a black theme. Skip the popup — too heavy. |

Regional names worth a look later for Arabic layout: Namshi (mobile-first, localized), RISK (Saudi streetwear), Riwaya (Dubai streetwear).

## What they all share

- Almost no colour: white or black background, the clothes carry the colour.
- One big photo at the top, then straight into products.
- Very little text, small uppercase labels, lots of empty space.
- Grid is 2 columns on phone, 3–4 on desktop, photos in a tall 4:5 frame.
- Header is only: logo, menu, search, bag.

## Direction for NO LOGO

**Pages**

1. Home — full-screen campaign photo or video, "New drop" row, categories, Instagram strip.
2. Shop — product grid with filter (category, size) and sort.
3. Product — big photo gallery, sizes, size guide, add to bag.
4. Bag — slide-in drawer.
5. Checkout — name, phone, city, address, notes. No online payment.
6. Order confirmed — order number + "we will contact you on WhatsApp".
7. Admin — products, stock per size, orders, order status.

**Order flow**

Customer checks out → order is saved → admin sees it in the panel → admin opens WhatsApp with the customer in one tap (message pre-filled with the order) → confirms and delivers.

**Languages**

Arabic (right-to-left) and English, switch in the header. Arabic is built as a real layout, not a translation on top. Most visitors will be on phones, so mobile is designed first.
